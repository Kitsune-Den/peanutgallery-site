Peanut Gallery is an AI co-host for streamers. A desktop app sits next to OBS, watches your stream, hears what you say when you push to talk, and reacts out loud through a voice and an animated overlay. Kitsune Den cloud (api.peanutgallery.gg) runs the AI and the voices for it. Installing the app, signing in, or subscribing means you accept these terms. If you don't, please don't use it.

The co-host speaks on your stream, so what it says goes out under your name. You're responsible for using it in a way that keeps your stream within Twitch's rules.

Plans and co-host time

Co-host time is the time the AI co-host is live on your stream. It counts by the minute while the app is live in full mode and your Twitch channel is live, plus any minute in which the cloud does paid work for you (an AI reply, a voiced line, a screen read, or chat read aloud in a gallery voice). Reactions mode, which plays the persona's pre-made clips, is free and unlimited.

  • Free: a one-time 3-hour trial of co-host time per Twitch channel, and reaction clips without limit. Chat read aloud in the standard voice is free up to 50,000 characters a month.
  • Bring your own AI: US$2.99 a month, 160 hours of co-host time, for your own AI with our voices and screen reading.
  • Hobby: US$9.99 a month, 60 hours of co-host time, with our AI and voices.
  • Professional: US$19.99 a month, 160 hours of co-host time, with our AI and voices.

Hours refill each month on the date you subscribed. Unused hours don't carry over. When you run out, the AI pauses and the app stays in reactions mode until your hours refill. Nothing is ever charged for going over: there are no overage fees.

To keep the service fair and its costs predictable, each account also has limits per minute (on AI replies, screen reads and voiced characters), can be live on one computer at a time, and shares daily budgets with everyone. If those budgets run out, the AI and voices pause for everyone until they reset; reactions mode keeps working.
