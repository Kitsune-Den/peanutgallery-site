Questions

What do I need to run it?

A PC running OBS Studio. Peanut Gallery is a small desktop app that sits next to OBS, and the co-host appears as a Browser Source in your scenes.

Will it slow down my stream?

It shouldn't. Your PC only takes the odd screenshot, plays the audio and shows the overlay. The heavy work (reading the screen, thinking, making the voice) happens on our servers, because your PC is busy enough already.

Which platforms does it work with?

Twitch at launch: it reads your chat, follows, raids, subs and channel-point redemptions. The overlay works in any OBS scene.

What does it see, and do you keep it?

A screenshot of your stream every 15 to 30 seconds, sent to our screen-reading service so the co-host knows what's happening. We don't store them. A "blind" switch pauses capture whenever something private is on screen.

Can chat make it say anything?

No. Chat is something the co-host reacts to, not a script it reads. Chat text-to-speech is off until you turn it on, works only through a channel-point reward or a command you choose, and every message is filtered, length-capped and rate-limited. Everything the co-host says passes a safety filter first, and a panic-mute hotkey silences it instantly.

Can I make my own co-host?

Yes. Each co-host is a folder with a personality, a voice and art, so you can tweak one of the five or build your own.

Does it work in Discord?

Not at launch. A Kitsune Den Discord bot that brings your co-host into voice chat and keeps it around between streams is planned for later.
